Frequently Asked Questions

How much do braces and Invisalign cost in Southampton & Wainscott?

Orthodontic treatment typically ranges from $3,000 to $7,000 depending on the complexity of your alignment. At Cartier Orthodontics, we make treatment comfortable for your family budget with flexible 0% interest financing and low monthly payment options. Your free consultation includes a complete, transparent cost breakdown before you commit to anything.

Is the initial orthodontic consultation really free?

Yes, 100% free! Your initial visit includes a comprehensive exam by Board Certified Orthodontist Dr. Michelle Cartier, digital 3D scanning, and a complete insurance verification with zero financial obligation.

How do I claim the $500 Promotional Offer?

Simply complete the form on this page or call our Southampton or Wainscott office to book your free consultation. Your $500 promotional credit will be applied directly to full braces or Invisalign treatment when you start care.

Do braces or Invisalign aligners hurt?

Modern braces and Invisalign SmartTrack aligners are designed for optimal comfort. You may experience slight pressure for a few days after adjustments or starting a new tray set, which indicates your teeth are safely aligning.

When should my child first see an orthodontist?

The American Association of Orthodontists (AAO) recommends an initial orthodontic evaluation by age 7. Early screening allows Dr. Cartier to guide natural jaw growth and prevent complex issues early on.

What is airway orthodontics and how does it help?

Airway orthodontics focuses on palate expansion and jaw development to ensure proper breathing pathways. It helps treat mouth breathing, snoring, and restless sleep in children and adults. Dr. Cartier collaborates directly with an onsite ENT specialist.

Do you accept dental insurance for orthodontic care?

Yes! We accept most major dental insurance plans. Our team will verify your orthodontic benefits during your free consultation and assist you with filing claims to maximize your coverage (often covering 25–50% of care).
